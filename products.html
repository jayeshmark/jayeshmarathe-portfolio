Built with Muse

The problem. Muse access is uneven, and the workflows worth using are hard to find in one place.

What it does. A platform around Meta Muse access, workflows, and discovery. Get or share an invite code, browse workflows by category, or join the waitlist.

What we’ve achieved. 247 invite codes in the pool, 1,108 clicks and 23,396 impressions in the last 28 days on Google Search (last checked Oct 2026), and $200 in sponsor revenue.

Try it at builtwithmuse.com →

Catalink

The problem. WhatsApp sellers need a clean catalog to share, but a full store setup is too heavy for the job.

What it does. A dead simple catalog tool: add products with a photo, name, and price, share one link, and buyers tap "I want this" to land in your chat. First month free, then Rs 500/month. Set up with Google sign-in.

What we’ve achieved. Talked to 3 users, and earned Rs 1,500 in revenue.

Try it at catalink.in →

RentNYC

The problem. NYC renters cannot easily tell what a fair rent looks like, or what their rights are.

What it does. A rent transparency map for NYC renters, with an AI assistant based on official NYC and New York State sources. The assistant gives general information, not legal advice.

What we’ve achieved. 145 real rents pinned by tenants, with live averages by bedroom count and neighbourhood. I built it end to end to practise product management on a real problem.

Try it at rentnyc.live →
