Case study, INZINT, Apr 2023 to Jan 2025

Closing $500K+ selling white label software, end to end.

Business Development Executive at INZINT. I managed SaaS deals end to end for US and global clients, from first outreach to signed SOW.

$500K+New revenue closed
7 clientsAcross the book
$35K to $45KTypical project size, plus dev staffing

Problem

Sell white label software to buyers who do not know INZINT, in a crowded market, and close deals that need custom scope, not a standard price list.

What I did

  • Owned full cycle deals: prospecting, qualification, proposals, and close.
  • Created custom proposals and SOWs for each client, instead of sending a generic deck.
  • Passed client insights back to internal teams to guide feature improvements.
  • Used outbound, LinkedIn and email, plus CRM discipline, to keep pipeline moving across multiple deals at once.

Tools used

Full cycle B2B salesProposalsSOWsCRMLinkedIn outreachEmail outreach

Result

$500K+ in new revenue closed, across 7 clients, with project sizes typically $35K to $45K plus dev staffing work. That result is from my own career record, not a client testimonial, and no client names are published here.

What I learned

Custom scope wins over generic pitching, and client insight is a product input, not just a sales note. That lesson carries into how I build products now: start from a real buyer problem, write the scope clearly, and iterate from what buyers actually say.
