Cold Email: 20 Outbound Plays You Can Hand to Your Agent

For a founder, sales rep, or small sales team doing outbound by email. Every play works on its own. Together they form one system: a subject line that earns the open, a first line about the prospect, a short body built around one problem, and a close that is easy to say yes to, followed by bumps and a breakup that close the sequence cleanly.

20 plays, each with a copy paste agent version. Informational guides and prompts only. They do not send emails, make calls, build lists, or book meetings for you. You still do the outreach yourself.

All 20 plays

  1. Write subject lines that look like internal email
  2. Use bumps and a breakup to close the sequence
  3. Keep cold email short, plain-text and mobile-first
  4. Write cold email with the REPLY method
  5. Open with a real observation, not a compliment or a merge tag
  6. Write executive cold emails with the Problem Prompter: one idea, unsure tone, peer proof, soft close
  7. Write the executive prospecting email line by line: objective, unsure problem, peer story, idea CTA, PS personalization
  8. Score cold emails on the five data backed elements
  9. Make the CTA something easy to say yes to, like samples
  10. Write cold email around pain, risk and offer, with trained personalisation
  11. Use email to reinforce the call, personalised to one or two points
  12. Keep first cold email short and mobile-readable
  13. Fit executive cold email to a nine-second scan
  14. Offer a peer insight instead of asking an executive for a meeting
  15. Use an interest call to action on cold email, a specific one in-deal
  16. Write cold emails in a four-part reason, problem, value, CTA framework
  17. Use the three-tier offer system to engage the 97% who are not in market
  18. Replace spray and pray templates with a few precise emails
  19. Keep cold email concise and 75% about their problem, 25% about you
  20. Cut cold emails to 75-120 words by deleting preamble and sourcing
